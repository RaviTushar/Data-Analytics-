## Introduction
Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by subscription-based businesses such as telecom companies, streaming services, SaaS platforms, and online subscription services. This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior data such as subscription details, usage activity, payment history, and engagement metrics to understand churn patterns and customer retention trends.

The project uses data analytics and machine learning techniques to study customer behavior and identify factors influencing customer churn.

---

## Problem Statement
Subscription-based businesses experience revenue loss due to customer churn. Understanding customer behavior and identifying patterns related to subscription cancellation is difficult because customer preferences and usage activity continuously change. Businesses require data-driven insights to better understand customer churn behavior and retention trends.
